SAVE THE TARA PIROHE!

Welcome! Welcome to Save the Tara Pirohe! Your mission is to help protect one of New Zealand's endangered native birds.

About the Bird

ENDANGERED

The tara pirohe, also called the black-fronted tern, is a rare bird that lives in New Zealand. It is mostly found near braided rivers in the South Island. It has a white body, grey wings and a black forehead. It eats small fish and insects.

Your Mission

Your job is to help the tara pirohe survive! You must:

  • 🐟 Collect food for the birds.
  • 🐾 Avoid dangerous predators.
  • 🥚 Protect the eggs from being eaten.
  • ♻️ Clean up rubbish near the river.
  • 🐣 Help baby birds grow up safely.
